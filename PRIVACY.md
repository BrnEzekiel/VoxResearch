# VoxResearch Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026.

VoxResearch (the Confluence macro app, the Rovo agent, and the VoxResearch web
app) is built by Brian Ezekiel for the Codegeist 2026 and Kiroween 2026
hackathons. Contact: https://brnezekiel.me/

## What we collect

- **Questions you ask.** Needed to generate answers. Recent questions and
  answers are cached for up to 7 days so repeats are instant; caches expire
  automatically.
- **Device fingerprints (web app only).** A random per-device identifier used
  solely to enforce the 10-message free quota. No accounts, names, or emails.
- **Page content you explicitly act on.** "Summarize this page" reads the
  current page; "Insert into page" writes the answer back. Nothing is read or
  written without you clicking.

## What we do NOT collect

No accounts, no names, no emails, no passwords, no payment data, no tracking
cookies, no analytics beacons, no sale of data to anyone, ever.

## Third parties

Questions are sent to Meta's Model API (api.meta.ai) to generate answers, and
the app runs on Atlassian Forge / Vercel infrastructure. Their own privacy
policies apply to data they process.

## Data retention and deletion

Cached answers expire automatically after 7 days. Quota counters persist while
the free tier exists. To request deletion of anything stored, contact
https://brnezekiel.me/ and it will be removed.
